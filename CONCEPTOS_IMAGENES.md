# Prompts de las imágenes conceptuales

Generadas con la herramienta de creación de imágenes integrada para la web Race Circuit. Son conceptos visuales, no capturas del juego. La idea común y los briefs de cada ambiente fueron:

## Estilo común

Use case: stylized-concept. Original unbranded sporty arcade cars, chunky black tires. Polished stylized 3D concept art, playful colors, landscape 16:9. No famous characters, no recognizable commercial game environments, no logos, no interface. These images are concepts, not screenshots.

## Escenario 1

Costa Turbo: Sunlit tropical coastal racing road, turquoise sea, palms, orange rocks and red-white curbs. Turquoise and orange cars racing around a bend, banana peel and blue turbo pickup. Warm afternoon.

## Escenario 2

Ciudad Neón: Friendly futuristic city at blue hour, violet and magenta architecture, cyan track edges and elevated bridges. Orange and turquoise cars racing through a curve.

## Escenario 3

Cumbre Helada: Bright snowy mountain circuit, rounded snowbanks, pine trees, orange-red curbs and a wooden bridge. Turquoise and orange cars, cool palette with warm accents.
